# Three-Statement Model: Meridian (Industrial Engineering)

**A fully linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement for Meridian, a fictional industrial engineering manufacturer, built from publicly sourced industry data.**

> Status: complete

## What the model does

- **Income statement** driven by revenue growth, gross margin and operating cost assumptions.
- **Balance sheet** with working capital (receivables, inventory, payables), fixed assets and depreciation, and debt.
- **Cash flow statement** derived from the other two statements, so the balance sheet balances every year.
- **Supporting schedules** for working capital, fixed assets and debt.

## Why it matters

A three-statement model is the base for budgeting, forecasting and valuation. Building it from a blank sheet, with every formula written by hand, tests how well the three statements link together.
