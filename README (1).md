# DCF Valuation and Capex Appraisal: Renewable Energy

**A discounted cash flow model for a fictional listed renewable energy company, used to value the business and to decide whether a new capital project is worth investing in.**

> Status: complete

## What the model does

1. **Company valuation.** Forecasts free cash flow, discounts it at the weighted average cost of capital (WACC) and adds a terminal value to reach enterprise and equity value.
2. **Capex project appraisal.** Evaluates a new generation project on its own cash flows using NPV, IRR and payback.
3. **Scenarios.** Shows how valuation and project returns change under different assumptions, such as power prices, construction cost and discount rate.

## Why renewable energy

The sector is capital-intensive, with large upfront investment and long-lived assets, so investment decisions depend heavily on discounting and on assumptions about future prices and costs.
